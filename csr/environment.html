Beyond compliance

Environment

Our environmental commitments run through the plant: fly ash recovery instead of landfill, a scrap-first feedstock strategy, and ongoing investment in cleaner, more efficient captive power.

Our hot-rolled product carries a GreenPro Ecolabel certified carbon footprint of 636 kg CO2eq per tonne (certificate valid to December 2027) — down from 797 kg in 2023, with a roadmap to 330 kg by 2030.

Regulatory transparency

Environmental Clearance Compliance

We operate under two Environmental Clearances (EC) and file half-yearly compliance reports with the regulatory authorities as a matter of standard practice — publicly available, not just filed and forgotten.

EC 2005

Half-yearly compliance reports filed continuously, including Oct 2023–Mar 2024, Apr–Sep 2024 and Oct 2024–Mar 2025.

EC 2009

Half-yearly compliance reports filed continuously, including Apr–Sep 2025 and Oct 2025–Mar 2026.

Public Disclosure

Compliance reports for both clearances are published for review, alongside our ISO 14001:2015 environmental management certification.
